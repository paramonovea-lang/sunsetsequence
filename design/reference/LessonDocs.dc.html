Rhythm and drums

The pulse, meter and note lengths, the drum kit, the beat lab and hi-hats.

01The pulse

Everything loops in cycles. s("bd*4") plays the sample bd (bass drum) four times per cycle: a four-on-the-floor kick. setcpm sets cycles per minute, so 124/4 gives 31 bars a minute, which is 124 BPM.

STRUDEL 
setcpm(124/4)
s("bd*4")

LISTEN FOR

A steady kick on every beat. The word bd lights up each time it plays.

TRY CHANGING

"bd*2" or "bd*8"
"bd(3,8)" 3 hits over 8 steps
setcpm(90/4)

01+Meter and note length

In Strudel and Tidal, meter and note length come from one rule: a cycle is one bar, and whatever you write inside the quotes shares that bar equally. Four items make four beats, three make three. To set the tempo for any meter, divide the BPM by the beats in the bar.

02The drum kit

Each line that starts with $: is a separate layer, and all layers play together. ~ is a rest, so "~ cp ~ cp" puts a clap on beats 2 and 4.

STRUDEL · highlighted lines are new 
setcpm(124/4)$: s("bd*4").bank("RolandTR909")$: s("~ cp ~ cp").bank("RolandTR909")$: s("[~ oh]*4").bank("RolandTR909").gain(.4)
