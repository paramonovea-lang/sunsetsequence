Quarrel walks down **A – G – F – E**, the "Andalusian" descent from flamenco, over a slow
four-on-the-floor kick. A clean guitar and a piano take turns playing the chords as broken notes,
a warm pad holds the long middle, and the song ends on a single gunshot.
