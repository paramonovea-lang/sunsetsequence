Crazy Breeze is a piano on its own, no drums: three four-chord loops in E minor,
**Em – G – D – Am**, **Em – D – C – D** and **G – Am – B – Em**, first as chords over a low
piano bass, then as quick broken chords. The 8-bar song plays three times, and each time the
last bar sounds darker.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `const melody_1 = "<Em G D Am>"` | One of the three chord loops; `< >` takes the next chord each time round. |
| `.dict("triads").mode("above").anchor("<E4 D4 D4 C4>")` | Plain three-note chords, built upward from the anchor note, so the hand stays in one place. |
| `n("0 <[7 3] [7 4] …>")` | Which notes of the chord play: `0` the lowest, `3`, `4` and `7` higher ones; `[7, 3]` with a comma plays two at once. |
| `.fast(2)` … `.fast(8)` | The same chords at 2× and 8× speed: from chords to running notes. |
| `.lpf("<5000 2000 800 300>")` | The last bar gets a filter that closes a little more each time through: 5000, 2000, then 800 Hz. |
| `arrange(...song, ...song, ...song)` | The 8-bar song (`2 + 1 + 2 + 1 + 1 + 1`) three times: 24 bars. |
