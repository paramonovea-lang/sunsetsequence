Hope is one chord loop, **C – Em – Am – F**, played by different instruments that come and go. The
song is a list of blocks: `arrange()` plays each block for its number of bars, from a filtered drum
intro to a piano-and-choir chorus and back out.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `const prog = "<C Em Am F>"` | The four chords, one per bar. Every instrument reads them from this one line. |
| `.dict("triads").mode("above").anchor("<C4 B3 C4 C4>")` | The piano plays plain three-note chords and keeps them close to middle C, so the hand barely moves. |
| `n("[0 1 2 3]*4").chord(prog).voicing()` | The same chords broken into notes, 16 per bar: the running piano of the chorus. |
| `.lpf(saw.slow(4).range(0, 5000))` | The intro's filter opens over 4 bars; the outro uses `isaw` to close it again. |
| `arrange([4, intro1], [8, intro2], …, [4, outro])` | The song: 4 + 8 + 8 + 4 + 4 + 4 + 4 = 36 bars. |
